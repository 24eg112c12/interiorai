Aesthetic Directions

Popular Interior Styles

Select an interior movement to inspire your next room overhaul.

Modern

Clean architectural lines, neutral backdrops, and functional understated elegance.

Minimalist

Decluttered serenity, intentional negative space, and organic natural light.

Traditional Indian

Carved teak woodwork, brass hardware, warm terracotta tones, and artisanal textiles.

Scandinavian

Blonde oak woods, cozy textures (hygge), soft pastels, and generous illumination.

Contemporary

Fluid curved silhouettes, high-contrast textures, and statement designer lighting.

Industrial

Exposed brickwork, matte black metal framing, raw timber, and loft aesthetics.

Luxury

Italian marble finishes, plush velvets, champagne gold accents, and grand chandeliers.

Bohemian

Macramé accents, lush tropical greenery, rattan cane furniture, and vibrant kilims.
