Discover architectural nuances, color palettes, and finish materials to choose the right aesthetic for your home makeover.

Crisp & Timeless

Modern

Born from early 20th-century functionalism, Modern interior design prioritizes clean geometric lines, clutter-free layouts, and honest materials. It uses muted monochromatic foundations accented by warm brass or black steel hardware.

Key Characteristics: Flat panel cabinetry, flush doors, recessed architectural spotlights, neutral warm gray and beige hues.

Clarity & Restraint

Minimalist

Focuses on "less is more." Spaces are characterized by open floor plans, abundant natural daylight, hidden storage cabinetry, and carefully curated furniture pieces where every object serves a distinct functional purpose.

Key Characteristics: Concealed handleless wardrobes, pure white/off-white lime wash walls, light oak timbers, zero visual clutter.
Design in Minimalist Style →
Heritage & Craft

Traditional Indian

Celebrates rich Indian heritage with hand-carved Teakwood or Sheesham furniture, antique brass accents, ornamental jali partition screens, vibrant silk/cotton tapestries, and serene pooja mandirs crafted with traditional joinery.

Key Characteristics: Terracotta tones, turmeric yellow, antique brass diyas, fluted wooden pilasters, intricate jhula swings.
Design in Traditional Indian Style →
Warmth & Nordic Simplicity

Scandinavian

Blends Nordic coziness (hygge) with bright illumination. Light natural birch and oak woods, plush bouclé textures, comfortable rounded chairs, and indoor flora create an inviting, refreshing domestic sanctuary.

Key Characteristics: Pastel accents, textured woolen throws, tapered wooden furniture legs, paper lantern pendants.
Design in Scandinavian Style →
Fluid & Forward-Looking

Contemporary

Current, in-the-moment design marked by organic curved sofas, ribbed fluted panels, statement monolithic coffee tables, and layered ambient lighting with warm linear LED profiles.

Key Characteristics: Curved architectural alcoves, micro-cement finishes, bronze tinted mirrors, sculptural lighting pendants.
Design in Contemporary Style →
Raw & Authentic

Industrial

Draws inspiration from urban loft architecture. Features exposed clay brickwork, matte black iron shelving, distressed leather seating, concrete flooring, and Edison filament bulb luminaires.

Key Characteristics: Exposed ducting, rustic reclaimed timber, steel mesh dividers, dark charcoal and terracotta color tones.
Design in Industrial Style →
Opulent & Sophisticated

Luxury

Exquisite high-end interiors featuring book-matched Italian marble, high-gloss PU lacquered surfaces, brushed champagne gold metal trim, plush velvet sectionals, and crystal chandeliers.

Key Characteristics: Statuary marble, recessed cove gold leafing, custom acoustic fabric wall cladding, automated ambient controls.
Design in Luxury Style →
Free-Spirited & Organic

Bohemian

Unconventional and eclectic. Layers handmade rattan furniture, woven jute rugs, macramé wall tapestries, brass accents, and vibrant botanical greenery for a deeply personal, lived-in sanctuary.

Key Characteristics: Cane furniture, leafy fiddle leaf figs, Turkish kilims, floor poufs, warm terracotta and moss green shades.
